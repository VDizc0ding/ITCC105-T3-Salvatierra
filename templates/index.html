03 / LEARNING REFLECTIONS

Looking back, moving forward.

01
PRELIM TERM

Starting with the BASICS

During the Prelim Term, I mostly learned the fundamentals of programming, particularly creating flowcharts and writing simple codes. We initially started with JavaScript during the first few weeks before transitioning to Python, which gave me the opportunity to become familiar with different programming languages. At first, understanding the logic behind coding and organizing instructions through flowcharts was something I had to get used to. However, as we continued with our activities, I gradually understood how these concepts worked together. This term helped me build a foundation in programming and understand that even complex systems begin with simple instructions and processes.
02
MIDTERM

More COMPLEX Systems

During the Midterm Term, our lessons became more challenging as we started developing more complex systems using Python. We explored different ways of organizing code and applying what we had learned to create programs with more functions and capabilities. One of the highlights of this term was preparing our project proposal, where we started thinking about how programming could be applied to an actual problem. This experience helped me understand that coding is not only about making a program work but also about planning, identifying problems, and finding possible solutions. It also allowed me to improve my problem-solving skills and become more confident in working with Python.
03
FINAL TERM

Applying what I've LEARNED

For the Final Term, most of our efforts were focused on preparing and developing our project proposal and research through Python. This was probably the term where I discovered more of my capabilities as a programmer, especially when experimenting with different features and finding ways to make our project possible. I also became familiar with vibe coding, which helped me explore ideas, build features, and understand how different parts of a system could work together. One of the things I particularly enjoyed was working with maps and learning how to incorporate mapping features into our project. Overall, this term allowed me to apply the knowledge I gained from the previous terms to something more practical. It made me realize that there is still so much I can learn and create through programming, especially when I take the initiative to explore beyond our classroom lessons.
